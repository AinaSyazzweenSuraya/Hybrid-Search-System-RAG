How to use Hybrid Search Engine

  1. Upload your documents.

    Choose PDF, Office, or text files. Scanned PDFs need selectable text before upload.

  2. Search a word or topic.

    Start with Hybrid. Keyword matches exact words; Meaning finds related concepts in your collection. Results depend on your documents.

  3. Check the source.

    Each match shows its document and section. Open the full section to read more.

Search returns source text, not an AI-written answer. My documents lets you remove files from the shared library.
